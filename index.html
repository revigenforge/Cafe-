ਖਾਣਾ The kitchen

A short menu,
cooked to order

Nothing sits under a lamp. The espresso bar runs all day; the kitchen sends out wraps, sliders and pasta from the same small counter.

  • Wrap & sliders

    A grilled tortilla wrap cut on the diagonal, a sesame brioche slider pinned with an olive, shoestring fries, and cocktail dip on the side. Enough for two if you're only a little hungry.

  • White sauce penne

    Penne folded through a cream sauce with broccoli, cherry tomatoes and oregano. Ask for it with chicken or keep it vegetarian — it's built either way.

  • Iced tea, poured over

    Brewed strong, chilled, then poured over ice so it layers dark to pale in the glass. Mint and a wedge of lemon on top. The one to order on the patio.

ਥਾਂ The space

Three ways to sit

You walk in through the gate, past the patio, into the hall. Pick whichever suits the weather and the company.

  1. At the gate

    The walkway

    Two lanterns, the sign in three scripts, and tables set down a checkered walkway under the roof. The quietest seats in the place, and the ones that catch the evening.

  2. Under the roof

    The patio

    Rattan chairs and glass-topped tables, screened with hedge and filled with palms. Covered overhead, open at the sides, fans running when it's warm.

  3. Inside

    The hall

    Air-conditioned, tiled, and kept spotless — the thing guests mention most. The counter runs warm along one wall with the machine at the end of it.

The counter

Everything comes
off one bar

The machine, the grinder, the syrups on the shelf behind it, and the cups stacked ready for takeaway. Order at the counter, sit anywhere, we'll bring it over.
