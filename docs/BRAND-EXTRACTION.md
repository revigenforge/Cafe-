## 2. Physical spaces (three distinct zones — a real structural asset for the site)

1. **Street entrance** — pink-cream textured wall, dark brown signboard with gold
   lettering, two black colonial lantern sconces, twin brown wooden gates opening
   onto a checkered grey/white tiled corridor with potted palms.
2. **Covered outdoor patio** — glass-topped brown rattan/wicker tables and chairs,
   translucent polycarbonate roof, artificial hedge screening, decorative
   ethnic-patterned yellow wall sconces, palms in white planters, wall fans,
   table QR code (digital menu already in use).
3. **Indoor air-conditioned hall** — fluted teak counter under a warm amber LED
   wash, black stone countertop, espresso machine + grinder, flavoured syrup
   bottles on floating wood shelves, three black cone pendants on a track, a
   recessed linear LED ceiling detail, glossy cream floor tiles, wooden tables
   with black chairs, framed vertical wall art.

Reviewers tagged the interior for **Cleanliness (2 mentions)** — worth a callout.

## 5. Reference layout (image 1) — section-by-section

Sticky top bar (contact strip) → dark nav with centred script wordmark and a
pill "Order Online" CTA → full-bleed hero with serif display headline over a
barista pouring milk → **angled/diagonal section dividers** (the signature
move of this design) → "Featured Drinks" three-card grid (image, title, blurb,
dark pill button) → two wide promo banners → "Daily Deal" band over a
coffee-bean background carrying a deal card, a food image and a newsletter
signup → four-column dark footer (about / menu / contact / social).

Type: high-contrast serif for display, sans for body. Feel: warm, dark, rich,
generous whitespace inside cream panels.
